## Band Profile: Fallow Haze

### Core Identity

- **Name:** Fallow Haze
- **Type:** Independent, home-studio-based recording project (not a live touring band).
- **Members & Roles:**
  - Nathan: Guitars, drums, vocals, mixing/production.
  - Jules: Bass, vocals.

### Musical Style & Sound

- **Genre:** Guitar-based pop / rock, Indie Rock.
- **Sonic Characteristics:** Driven by catchy melodies, gritty and driving guitars, melodic basslines, and authentic, real-instrument arrangements.
- **Influences:** The Rolling Stones, The Beatles, The Who, Tom Petty, Fastball.
- **Ethos:** Combining a classic, raw rock edge with tight, memorable pop hooks. Total DIY production with high sonic standards.

### Content & Visuals

- **Platform:** YouTube (Main audio/video releases and supporting YouTube Shorts).
- **Visual Style:** High-quality, slick, self-produced music videos filmed entirely at home using natural lighting and studio spaces.
- **Release Format:** Consistent, high-output singles paired with performance-driven music videos.

### Recent Milestones & Context

- **Current Campaign:** Successfully completed a high-momentum creative sprint, writing, recording, producing, and filming 5 fully finished tracks in 5 consecutive months.
- **Latest Single:** "Chasing the Sun" (Track 5 of the 5-month release cycle).
